Our Rafting Trips

Los Dientes del Diablo

Take on the exciting rapids of Los Dientes del Diablo. This adventure combines thrilling river action with beautiful scenery and is perfect for groups looking for an unforgettable experience.

Difficulty: Advanced

Duration: 5 hours

Big Mallard

Big Mallard offers an exciting rafting experience with a mixture of fast-moving water and calmer sections. It is a great choice for friends and families who want adventure without an extreme challenge.

Difficulty: Moderate

Duration: 3 hours

Half Day Adventure

Enjoy a fun introduction to white water rafting with our Half Day Adventure. This trip provides plenty of time on the river while giving beginners a chance to enjoy the outdoors at a comfortable pace.

Difficulty: Beginner

Duration: 2.5 hours
